# Multi-Timeframe Cryptocurrency Analysis (12H-6H-4H Basis)

This skill provides systematic technical analysis for cryptocurrency pairs using 12-hour, 6-hour, and 4-hour timeframes to identify trend convergence, divergence, and high-probability trading setups.

## 📊 **Analysis Framework**

### **Timeframe Hierarchy**
- **12H**: Primary trend direction and major support/resistance
- **6H**: Intermediate trend and momentum confirmation  
- **4H**: Entry timing and short-term momentum

### **Required Data**
- 250 candles for each timeframe (12H, 6H, 4H)
- Current price from live ticker
- Volume profile analysis
- Key technical indicators (EMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands)

## 🔍 **Step-by-Step Analysis Process**

### **1. 12H Timeframe Analysis (Primary Trend)**
- Identify major trend direction (bullish/bearish/sideways)
- Locate major support/resistance zones
- Analyze 12H EMA 50/200 crossover
- Check 12H RSI for overbought/oversold conditions
- Identify chart patterns (triangles, flags, wedges)

### **2. 6H Timeframe Analysis (Intermediate Confirmation)**
- Confirm 12H trend alignment or identify divergence
- Analyze 6H momentum indicators
- Locate intermediate support/resistance
- Check for trend continuation/reversal patterns
- Volume analysis on 6H candles

### **3. 4H Timeframe Analysis (Entry Timing)**
- Fine-tune entry points based on higher timeframe bias
- Identify 4H pullback opportunities in trend direction
- Analyze 4H candlestick patterns for entry signals
- Check 4H indicator alignments
- Risk/reward assessment for potential entries

## 🎯 **Convergence/Divergence Analysis**

### **Bullish Confluence Signals**
- 12H: Uptrend + price above EMA 50/200
- 6H: Higher highs/higher lows + bullish momentum
- 4H: Pullback to support with bullish reversal signals
- Volume increasing on up days across timeframes

### **Bearish Confluence Signals**
- 12H: Downtrend + price below EMA 50/200  
- 6H: Lower highs/lower lows + bearish momentum
- 4H: Rally to resistance with bearish rejection signals
- Volume increasing on down days across timeframes

### **Divergence Warnings**
- Price making higher highs but indicators showing lower highs
- Volume decreasing on trend moves
- Timeframe disagreement on trend direction
- RSI divergence across multiple timeframes

## 📈 **Technical Indicators Used**

### **Moving Averages**
- EMA 20: Short-term momentum
- EMA 50: Medium-term trend
- EMA 200: Long-term trend direction

### **Oscillators**
- RSI (14): Overbought (>70) / Oversold (<30) conditions
- MACD: Trend momentum and signal line crossovers
- Stochastic: Momentum exhaustion signals

### **Volatility**
- Bollinger Bands (20,2): Support/resistance and squeeze detection
- ATR: Volatility measurement for stop placement

## 💡 **Execution Framework**

### **Trend Following Entries**
- Enter on 4H pullbacks in direction of 12H/6H trend
- Wait for 4H candlestick confirmation (pin bar, engulfing, etc.)
- Use 6H momentum for entry timing confirmation

### **Counter-Trend Entries (Higher Risk)**
- Only consider when all timeframes show exhaustion
- Look for divergence signals across 2+ timeframes
- Require strong reversal candlestick patterns
- Tighter stop losses due to counter-trend nature

### **Risk Management**
- Position size based on 12H volatility (ATR-based)
- Stop loss placement beyond 12H swing points
- Profit targets at 12H/6H support/resistance levels
- Risk-reward minimum 1:2 for trend trades, 1:1.5 for counter-trend

## 🚫 **Avoid These Conditions**
- Choppy/sideways movement across all timeframes
- Conflicting signals (12H bullish, 6H bearish, 4H bullish)
- Low volume on breakout attempts
- Major news events pending without volatility contraction

## 📊 **Performance Metrics to Track**
- Win rate by timeframe alignment
- Average profit/loss per trade
- Maximum consecutive wins/losses
- Time-in-market efficiency
- Drawdown analysis
